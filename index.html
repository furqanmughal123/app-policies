Privacy Policy for WordSnap

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how WordSnap ("we," "our," or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application.

No Data Collection

WordSnap does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or device data. The app does not require you to create an account, does not request access to sensitive permissions, and does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Storage

Any information generated by your use of the app (such as game progress or preferences) is stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and is removed if you uninstall the app.

Children's Privacy

WordSnap does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, because the app does not collect data from any user.

Third-Party Services

WordSnap does not integrate with any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.

Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change in the future, we will update this policy and revise the "Last updated" date above.
